### Interview Talking Points

**Question:** "Tell me about a project you're proud of."

**Answer:**
> "I'd love to talk about 'When Does My Kid Dance?'. As a parent at dance competitions, I saw firsthand how difficult it was to track multiple kids across complex schedules. Parents were frantically searching through 50-page PDFs on their phones.
>
> I built a web app with two main features: dancer search with color-coding, and a filterable full schedule. The key UX decision was the color-coding—each dancer gets a unique color, making it instantly recognizable whose schedule you're looking at.
>
> The result? 85% mobile usage, which validated my mobile-first approach, and real adoption by studios. Parents tell me it 'saved their sanity,' which is the best feedback I could ask for.
>
> The technical stack is Next.js with TypeScript, using static data generation for instant load times. You can see the full case study on my portfolio."
