# Professional Headshot Setup

## Where to Add Your Photo

Place your professional headshot in the `public` folder with the name `headshot.jpg`

**File Path:** `public/headshot.jpg`

---

## Photo Requirements

### Technical Specs:
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Dimensions:** At least 800x800px (square)
- **File Size:** Under 500KB (optimized for web)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 1:1 (square)

### Photo Guidelines:

#### ✅ DO:
- Use a professional headshot
- Smile and look approachable
- Make eye contact with camera
- Use good lighting (natural light or studio)
- Wear business casual attire
- Use a clean, simple background
- Make sure photo is recent (within 1-2 years)
- Use high resolution image

#### ❌ DON'T:
- Use casual vacation photos
- Include other people
- Use low quality/blurry images
- Have busy/distracting backgrounds
- Wear overly casual clothing (tank tops, etc.)
- Use heavily filtered or edited photos
- Include sunglasses or hats
